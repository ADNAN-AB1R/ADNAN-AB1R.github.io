This is a demo, not the Dhaka model. It's a toy mobsim written from scratch for this page, on a synthetic grid city: agents hold a home → work plan, cars slow down as links fill up (BPR), and after each simulated morning 15% of drivers re-route on the travel times they experienced, so the iterations relax. The real Dhaka scenario runs in MATSim — see how it's built.
